Spotify needs a free developer app (one-time, about two minutes): open the Spotify developer dashboard, create an app, add as a Redirect URI, tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, then paste its Client ID here.

The visualizer becomes a Spotify Connect speaker, so you can pick songs from the Spotify app on your phone. In-page playback needs Spotify Premium.

Video and playlist links both work. Some videos don't allow embedding.

Capture PC audio: choose Entire screen and turn on Share system audio. A phone can stream to this PC over Bluetooth or AirPlay, or plug into the line-in.

How the music gets in, and privacy
  • Spotify and YouTube: the music plays inside this page. Their audio is protected, so the page can't read it directly; instead it listens to its own tab. When Chrome asks, pick This tab and keep Share tab audio on. It's one click per session.
  • Spotify from your phone: after connecting, open Spotify on the phone, tap the speaker icon and choose 3D Music Visualizer, then press Play here & listen (or just Listen) on this page.
  • Any other app: Other sources → Capture PC audio with "Share system audio" on.

Keys: 1–5 styles · ← → previous/next · Space play/pause · A auto-cycle · F full view · Esc exit. Audio is only analyzed in this page. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Service sign-ins stay in this browser.
