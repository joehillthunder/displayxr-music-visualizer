How to visualize music from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or your phone
  • Music playing on this PC (any app or browser tab): click Capture PC audio, choose Entire screen, and turn on Share system audio. To follow one browser tab only, pick that tab and turn on Share tab audio.
  • Music on your phone or another device: stream it to this PC over Bluetooth (Windows can act as a Bluetooth speaker), AirPlay or Spotify Connect, then use Capture PC audio. Or plug the device into the line-in, pick it from the list, and click Use input.
  • A room or a live set: pick a microphone and click Use input.
  • No music handy: Demo loop plays a built-in lo-fi beat.

Keys: 1–5 styles · ← → previous/next · A auto-cycle · F full view · Esc exit. Captured audio is only analyzed in this page. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
